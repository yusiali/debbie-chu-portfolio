The Cyber Directive

Founder & Editor-in-Chief

Academic Journal Cybersecurity Policy Law & Technology

A national, interdisciplinary student journal on cybersecurity technology, policy, and law. The first edition is in editing and is scheduled for spring.

Questions of jurisdiction, attribution, and risk rarely fit neatly into a computer-science journal or a law review. The Cyber Directive publishes the work in between.
