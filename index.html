About

Third-year at UT Austin in Government and Plan II Honors. I study public policy, cybersecurity, and American civic culture. I am an Undergraduate Cybersecurity Fellow at the Strauss Center, a research assistant at the Clark Center for Australian & New Zealand Studies, and founder of CyberServe USA, a national nonprofit teaching digital safety to underserved communities.

My writing has appeared in The Texas Horn and The Texas Orator. I am editing the inaugural issue of The Cyber Directive, a national undergraduate journal on cybersecurity, policy, and law. I plan to practice as a federal cybersecurity lawyer and serve in government.
